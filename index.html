Play with friends in other cities

Every player uses their own phone or laptop. No shared screen needed.

  1. Open the game linkEveryone opens Yaari Brawl on their own phone or laptop, wherever they are.
  2. Tap “Online vs friend”Pick your fighter at the top of the arena.
  3. One of you hostsTap “Host a match” and send your friend the 4-letter code on WhatsApp.
  4. Join and fightYour friend types the code and taps Join. The host taps Fight! Rematch as often as you like.

Matches are one on one. With more of you online, several pairs can host their own matches at the same time.
